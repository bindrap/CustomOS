## Color Scheme:

- **PARTEEK ASCII Art**: Cyan (bright blue)
- **Borders**: Purple
- **Title "Custom Arch Linux - By Parteek"**: Green with white stars
- **Feature highlights**: Yellow
- **Installation section**: Cyan headers, Green/White text
- **Keybindings**: Green with white arrows
- **Disk info**: Cyan label, Yellow disk info
- **Final prompt**: Green "Ready to install?" with white command

## What's Different:

1. ✨ **Large "PARTEEK" ASCII art** at the top in cyan
2. 🎨 **Full color scheme** throughout
3. ✨ **Emojis** for visual appeal
4. 📚 **Feature showcase** - shows what users get after installation
5. ⌨️ **Keybinding preview** - highlights the main shortcuts
6. 🎯 **Clear call-to-action** - "Ready to install? Type: install-arch"

## To see it:

1. Build a new ISO: `./build-iso-simple.sh`
2. Boot in VirtualBox
3. Enjoy the colorful welcome screen!
